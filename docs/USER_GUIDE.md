# EPFO Enforcement Officer (EO) Tour Diary - User Manual & Guide

Welcome to the **EPFO EO Tour Diary & Field Inspection Portal**. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for Enforcement Officers (EO/AO), APFCs, and Regional Administrators.

---

## Key Modules & Navigation

### 1. Dashboard (`/dashboard`)
The primary executive dashboard provides quick insights into:
- **Today's Field Visits**: Immediate schedule for current day visits.
- **Section 7A / 14B Recovery Progress**: Progress gauge tracking annual dues collection vs ₹5,00,000 target.
- **High-Priority Follow-ups**: Action items requiring urgent attention.
- **Recent Activity Feed**: Timeline stream of inspection logs, tour approvals, and claims.

---

### 2. Tour Program Schedule & Daily Diary Builder (`/tours`)
- **Propose Tour Schedule**: Click `+ Propose Tour Schedule` to submit a monthly tour itinerary to APFC for approval.
- **Daily Diary Builder**: Use the accordion-style builder to log multi-entry daily inspection visits.
- **Auto-Save Engine**: Your work automatically saves as a local draft every 1.5 seconds.
- **GPS & Camera Check-in**: Click `GPS` for automatic location coordinates or `Camera` to capture site photos.

---

### 3. Field Inspection Logs & Inline-Editable Visit Table (`/inspections`)
- View and manage inspection logs in an inline-editable grid.
- **10 Row Action Buttons**: `Add`, `Edit`, `Save`, `Delete`, `PDF`, `Call`, `Upload`, `Navigate`, `History`, `Duplicate`.
- Sticky backdrop-blur table header for scrolling across long lists.

---

### 4. Pending Compliance Follow-ups (`/followups`)
- Track pending work items across 4 status tabs (`PENDING`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`, `OVERDUE`).
- Assign priority badges (🔴 High, 🟡 Medium, 🔵 Low).
- Schedule next field visit dates directly from action cards.

---

### 5. Employer Communication Hub (`/communication`)
- Direct 1-click communication tools:
  - 📞 **Call**: Launches native phone dialer (`tel:`).
  - 💬 **WhatsApp**: Opens chat with pre-filled EPFO compliance templates (ECR Notice, Section 7A Hearing, Form 11 Notice).
  - ✉️ **SMS**: Launches SMS app with pre-formatted reminder text.
  - 📋 **Copy Number**: Copies phone number to clipboard.
  - 📝 **Log Discussion Note**: Records call notes in the audit log timeline.

---

### 6. Digital Document Vault (`/documents`)
- Store and manage inspection files organised by establishment code directories (`/OR-6276/`, `/OR-BBS-1238/`, `/GENERAL/`).
- Supports PDFs, photos, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, and ZIP archives.
- Revision versioning (`v1.0`, `v1.1`) with 1-click file replacement and deletion.

---

### 7. Comprehensive Reports & Analytics Engine (`/reports` & `/analytics`)
- Export official monthly reports as **Printable PDF** or downloadable **CSV Data**.
- View visual charts for monthly visit trends, district-wise volume, employer compliance, and YoY annual comparisons.
